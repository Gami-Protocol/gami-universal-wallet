## App Name

## Subtitle

## Promotional Text
Turn everyday app activity into XP, quests, rewards, and onchain progress with Gami Protocol.

## Description
Gami Wallet is the universal rewards wallet for Gami Protocol. Connect your wallet, complete quests, track XP, unlock tiers, and claim rewards across connected apps and partner campaigns.

Gami helps users build a portable rewards identity across apps, games, communities, and Web3 campaigns.

### Key Features
- Connect your wallet securely
- Sign in with Ethereum
- Track XP and universal points
- Complete quests and unlock rewards
- View reward history and activity
- Build streaks and progress through tiers
- Participate in partner campaigns

## Review Notes
Gami Wallet is a non-custodial rewards and engagement wallet. The app allows users to connect a wallet, sign in, view XP, complete quests, and claim in-app rewards. The app does not promise financial returns and does not provide trading, investment advice, or custodial services.
